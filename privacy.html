1. Who we are

Kivora Tech Pvt. Ltd. ("Kivora", "we", "us") is an engineering firm registered in Nepal, registration number to be confirmed, with its office at Kathmandu Tech Park, Kathmandu, Nepal. We are the data controller for the personal data described here.

Where we process personal data only on a client's behalf, that client is the controller and we act as their processor under a written data processing agreement.

2. What we collect

What you give us

  • Enquiries. When you use the contact form we receive the name, email address and message you submit. That form opens your own email client, so the message travels through your mail provider rather than our servers.
  • Engagement records. For client work we hold your staff's contact details, project scope, and records needed to deliver and evidence the work.
  • Job applications. We hold your application and correspondence for as long as the process is live, and as needed afterwards.

What is collected automatically

  • Server logs. Our host records IP address, timestamp, requested URL, user agent and referrer, used for security, abuse prevention and diagnosing faults.
  • No advertising trackers. This site sets no advertising cookies and loads no remote scripts, fonts or images from third-party CDNs beyond Google Fonts.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for special category data — health, biometrics, political opinions, religious belief, sex life or sexual orientation — through this website. Please do not include such information in an enquiry.

5. Cookies

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies and runs no third-party tracking. Two things worth noting, neither of which is an advertising cookie:

  • Web fonts are served by Google Fonts, which means Google sees your IP address when the page loads. A content blocker will stop the request, at the cost of falling back to system fonts.
  • Session and security cookies may be set by our host to serve and protect the site.

6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become engagements: 12 months, then deleted.
  • Client and engagement records: the contract term plus 7 years for audit, tax and warranty duties.
  • Security logs: 12 months, longer where an investigation is open.
  • Unsuccessful applications: 12 months, or longer if you ask.

7. Security

We apply to our own systems the standard we would apply to yours: least-privilege access, encryption in transit and at rest, centralised logging, and continuous patching. More detail on the security page.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data and is likely to put you at risk, we will notify you and the relevant authority without undue delay, and within 72 hours where the law requires it.

8. Your rights

Subject to the law applying to you, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold, correct inaccurate data, have it erased, restrict or object to processing, receive it in a portable format, withdraw consent at any time, and complain to your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these, email info@kivoratech.com.np. We aim to respond within 30 days and may need to verify your identity first.

9. International transfers

We are headquartered in Nepal and work with clients abroad, so personal data may be processed outside Nepal, including in the EEA, United States, Singapore, India where a client or subprocessor is located.

Where data leaves the EEA we rely on an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses, with a transfer risk assessment and supplementary measures where needed. A copy is available on request.

10. Children

Our services are for organisations, not children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy as our practices or the law change. The date at the top always reflects the current version. For material changes affecting existing clients we will give notice by email or a prominent notice on this site.
