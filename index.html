Five steps, and you can stop at any of them

Engagements start with a paid-for discovery you own the output of. If the answer is that you do not need us, you keep the document and we part friends.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We read your code, your infrastructure and your incident history. You get a written assessment of where the real risk and the real cost sit.

  2. 02

    Architecture

    A design you could hand to any competent team, with the trade-offs written down rather than implied. This is the deliverable you keep.

  3. 03

    Build

    Short iterations against real acceptance criteria. You see running software every week, not status reports.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Operate

    Optional, and the part most clients skip. On-call, alerting and incident response with agreed response times.

What being small actually gets you

Most of what firms sell you is scale. Here is what ours gives you instead.

You get the senior person

There is no bench of juniors behind the conversation. The engineer who reviews your design is the one who would maintain it — and there is no handover because there is nobody to hand you to.

Decisions in a day, not a quarter

A three-person company can change its mind in a day. When a design turns out to be wrong we fix it this week instead of raising a change order next quarter.

Nothing to lock you in

No proprietary platform, no annual minimum, no licence to lose. The code is in your repo and the infrastructure is in your cloud account. If we stop, nothing breaks.

We tell you when you do not need us

If a three-week fix solves it, we will say so instead of selling a six-month programme. That costs us revenue and is the reason clients come back.

Engineers, not account managers

The first call is with whoever will do the work. There is no solution-architect layer translating between you and the person writing the code.

We build to controls we cannot certify

We are not SOC 2 certified, and we say so. We still apply least-privilege access, encryption, and audited change history to your systems — the work is the same, the paperwork is not there yet.
